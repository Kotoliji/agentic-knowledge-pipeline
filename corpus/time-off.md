# Time Off

This page explains paid time off, sick leave and other leave at Halden Systems.

## Paid time off

Full-time employees get 26 days of paid time off per calendar year, in addition to public holidays in their country of employment. Part-time employees get a pro-rated amount. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year; carried-over days expire on 31 March.

Request time off in the PeopleHub app. Requests of up to 3 days need at least one week's notice. Requests longer than 3 days need at least four weeks' notice. Your manager approves or declines the request within 3 working days.

## Sick leave

If you are sick, tell your manager before 10:00 on the first day and record the absence in PeopleHub. For absences longer than 3 consecutive days, a doctor's note is required. Sick leave does not reduce your paid time off.

## Parental leave

Birth parents receive 20 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Other parents receive 10 weeks of fully paid parental leave, which can be taken in up to three blocks within the first year after the birth or adoption.

## Other leave

Employees can take 2 paid days per year for volunteering with a registered charity. Bereavement leave is 5 paid days for the loss of a close family member.
