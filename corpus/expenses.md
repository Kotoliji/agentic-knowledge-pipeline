# Expense Policy

Halden Systems reimburses reasonable business expenses that employees pay out of pocket. This policy covers what can be claimed, the limits, and how claims are approved.

## Submitting a claim

Claims are submitted in the Ledgerline app. Every claim needs an itemised receipt; a card statement alone is not accepted. Claims must be submitted within 60 days of the purchase date. Claims older than 60 days are rejected automatically and cannot be appealed.

Reimbursements are paid with the next monthly payroll run after approval. Payroll runs on the 25th of each month.

## Approval

Claims up to 250 EUR are approved by your direct manager. Claims above 250 EUR also need approval from the Finance team, which usually takes up to five working days. Purchases above 1,000 EUR must be pre-approved by Finance before you buy; without pre-approval they are not reimbursed.

## Travel

Book flights and hotels through the TravelDesk portal so they are billed to the company directly. Economy class is the default for flights under six hours. Business class is allowed for flights of six hours or longer.

The hotel limit is 160 EUR per night in most cities and 220 EUR per night in London, Paris, Zurich and New York.

## Meals and per diem

When travelling for work you receive a per diem of 45 EUR per day for meals instead of submitting individual meal receipts. Team dinners need prior approval from your manager and are capped at 60 EUR per person, including drinks.

## Not reimbursable

Alcohol outside approved team dinners, traffic and parking fines, upgrades to business class on short flights, and personal entertainment are never reimbursed.
