# Remote Work

Halden Systems is a hybrid company. This page explains where you can work from and what equipment support is available.

## Working location

Employees can work remotely up to 3 days per week. Teams agree on one shared office day per week so that everyone on the team is in the office together. Fully remote contracts are available only for roles that are advertised as fully remote.

Working from another country is allowed for up to 30 days per calendar year, as long as the country is in the EU or the UK. Longer stays, or any stay outside the EU and the UK, need prior approval from HR because of tax and insurance rules.

## Home office equipment

New employees receive a one-time home office stipend of 500 EUR for a desk, chair or monitor. Receipts are submitted as a normal expense claim. Company laptops and headsets are provided separately and do not count towards the stipend.

## Internet and coworking

The company pays 30 EUR per month towards home internet for employees who work remotely at least 2 days per week. Employees who live more than 50 km from an office can use a coworking space, up to 200 EUR per month, paid through an expense claim.

## Working hours

Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in your local time zone. Outside core hours you can arrange your day as you like, as long as you attend your team's meetings.
