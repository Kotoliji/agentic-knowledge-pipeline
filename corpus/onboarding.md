# Onboarding Guide

Welcome to Halden Systems. This guide covers your first weeks: equipment, accounts, people and the checkpoints you can expect.

## Before your first day

Your laptop is shipped to your home address five working days before your start date. If it has not arrived two days before you start, contact it-help@halden.example. You will receive a personal email with a link to activate your company account; the link is valid for 72 hours.

## First week

On your first day you join the welcome session at 09:30 CET, where IT sets up multi-factor authentication with you. Every new employee is assigned an onboarding buddy from another team. Your buddy meets you at least twice a week during your first month and is your first contact for questions that are not about your actual work.

During the first week you complete the mandatory security training and the data protection training in the Learnwell portal. Both must be completed within 5 working days of your start date.

## First months

Your manager sets three onboarding goals with you in your first week. You have a check-in with your manager after 30 days and a formal review after 90 days. The probation period is 6 months; for engineering roles it can be shortened to 3 months if the 90-day review is positive.

## Getting help

Use the #ask-anything chat channel for general questions. IT support is available at it-help@halden.example from 08:00 to 18:00 CET on working days.
